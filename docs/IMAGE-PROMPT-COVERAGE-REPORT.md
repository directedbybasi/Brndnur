## 3. Analysis of Intentionally Image-Free Sections

The audit confirmed **138 section modules** across the website that intentionally contain no photographic imagery. These sections fall into distinct, purposeful categories:

1. **Global Header & Navigation:** Clean typographic navbar with SVG brand logo anchor.
2. **Global Site Footer:** High-contrast navy footer with legal registration details, WhatsApp badges, and text navigation columns.
3. **Accordion FAQ Systems:** High-density text accordion grids on `/faqs/`, `/trademark-registration/`, and individual service pages designed for rapid reading and zero visual distractions.
4. **Government Statutory Fee & Pricing Comparison Tables:** Strict typographic comparison matrices on `/packages/` and `/trademark-classes/` where tables ensure complete legal clarity.
5. **Step-by-Step Procedural Roadmaps:** Numbered typographic cards (e.g., Steps 1–4 on `/how-it-works/`) where numbered typography and badge elements guide the founder step-by-step.
6. **Statutory Legal & Policy Declarations:** Formal legal prose on `/privacy-policy/`, `/terms-and-conditions/`, `/disclaimer/`, and `/refund-cancellation-policy/` where decorative imagery would undermine legal seriousness.
