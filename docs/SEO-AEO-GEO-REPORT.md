## 1. Technical SEO Audit & Infrastructure

### 1.1 Canonical URLs & Origin Consistency
All 32 pages enforce strict canonical tags pointing to `https://brndnur.com/[slug]/` with a trailing slash, matching the static folder structure generated by Astro.
- No protocol mismatches (`http://` vs `https://`).
- No hostname variations (`www.brndnur.com` vs `brndnur.com`).
- Enforced via Apache `.htaccess` rewrites and `BaseLayout.astro` canonical tags.

### 1.2 Title & Meta Description Audit
- **Unique Titles:** 32 distinct titles verified. Every page incorporates a relevant keyword topic, descriptive service name, and the standard brand suffix `| BRNDNUR Advisory LLP`.
- **Meta Descriptions:** All 32 pages feature unique, factual descriptions ranging between 120 and 160 characters, highlighting target inclusions, applicant categories, and actionable guidance without keyword stuffing.

### 1.3 Heading Hierarchy (H1 through H6)
- **Single H1:** Each page contains exactly one `<h1>` specifying the primary topic or service.
- **Strict Sequential Order:** Prior audits revealed a level skip from `<h2>` to `<h4>` caused by footer navigation headings. This was resolved by converting footer headings to `<p class="bn-foot-title">`. Additionally, `404.astro` was corrected from `<h1> -> <h3>` to `<h1> -> <h2>`.
- **Result:** Automated crawl verification confirms **0 heading skips** across all 32 pages.

### 1.4 XML Sitemap & Robots Directive
- `dist/sitemap.xml`: Formatted according to sitemaps.org standards, listing all 31 canonical public routes. The error page `/404.html` is intentionally excluded.
- `dist/robots.txt`:
  ```text
  User-agent: *
  Allow: /
  Sitemap: https://brndnur.com/sitemap.xml
  ```
  Grants open crawl access to all search and AI crawlers while directing search bots directly to the authoritative sitemap.

Answer Engine Optimization ensures that search engine answer boxes, voice search assistants, and featured snippets can parse and extract direct answers to high-intent queries.

The core service and educational pages are structured around direct query headings:

## 4. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI Search Discovery

GEO prepares the website for discovery, citation, and accurate factual representation by generative AI search platforms (ChatGPT Search, Claude, Perplexity, Google Gemini).

### 4.1 Deployment of `llms.txt`
To serve AI crawlers and LLMs with clear, token-efficient, authoritative context, `public/llms.txt` was authored and built into `dist/llms.txt`.

Key contents of `llms.txt`:
1. **Core Organization Profile:** Concise, non-promotional summary of BRNDNUR Advisory LLP as an independent brand protection consultancy in Calicut, Kerala.
2. **Statutory Non-Affiliation Disclaimer:** Explicitly clarifies that BRNDNUR is a private consultancy and not a government office, court, or the official Trade Marks Registry.
3. **Service Directory & Direct URLs:** Index of canonical pages for searches, filings, classifications, renewals, and business setup.
4. **Verified Contact Channels:** Canonical phone (`+91 90481 88899`), email, and website.

### 4.2 AI Crawler Access Policy
- **Directive:** Crawlers from OpenAI (`GPTBot`), Anthropic (`ClaudeBot`), Google (`Google-Extended`), and Perplexity (`PerplexityBot`) are explicitly allowed full access in `robots.txt` (`User-agent: * Allow: /`).
- **Rationale:** Allowing AI crawlers ensures BRNDNUR's services, transparent fee policies, and legal disclaimers are accurately cited when entrepreneurs in Kerala and across India query AI assistants for trademark guidance.
