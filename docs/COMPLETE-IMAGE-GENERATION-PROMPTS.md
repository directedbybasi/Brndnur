## 1. Commercial Photography Art Direction & 13-Point Prompting Standard

Every prompt in this library is engineered as a complete, self-contained creative brief ready to paste directly into high-fidelity image-generation models (Midjourney v6, FLUX.1 Pro, DALL-E 3, or Imagen 3). All 78 prompts adhere strictly to these 13 photographic criteria:

1. **Exact Intended Subject:** Concrete depiction tailored precisely to the section's business and legal message.
2. **Composition & Hierarchy:** Explicit foreground, middle ground, background arrangement with intentional negative space for UI text overlay.
3. **Camera & Optical Systems:** High-end medium format (Hasselblad H6D-100c, Leica S3, Fujifilm GFX 100 II) and flagship full-frame (Sony A1, Canon EOS R5, Nikon Z9) camera aesthetics with dedicated prime lenses (35mm, 50mm, 75mm, 85mm, 90mm macro, 45mm tilt-shift) and authentic f-stops.
4. **Physical Lighting:** Plausible natural daylight—soft directional morning window sun, diffused northern daylight, and gentle studio key lights with realistic shadow falloff.
5. **Tactile Materials & Textures:** 400gsm deckle-edge cotton rag paper, hand-stitched leather folios, dark Kerala teak, honed limestone, polished marble, and aged bronze metalwork.
6. **BRNDNUR Brand Palette:** Organic integration of warm Ivory (`#F4EFE4`), deep midnight Navy (`#13233A`), and subtle brushed Gold/Bronze (`#A8781A`).
7. **Regional Authenticity:** Authentic Indian founders, craftspeople, and advisors in natural Kerala business settings (Kozhikode, Kochi, Calicut), avoiding stock-photo stereotypes.
8. **Professional Art Direction:** Premium commercial and editorial photography appropriate to an elite brand advisory firm.
9. **Physical Realism:** Natural proportions, realistic skin micro-textures, believable hand anatomy, and coherent geometric perspective.
10. **Framing & Aspect Ratios:** Exact container alignment (4:3, 16:9, 3:2, 4:5, 5:4, 3:4) eliminating Cumulative Layout Shift.
11. **Text Restrictions:** Explicit instruction of zero legible text, zero fake government certificates, and zero garbled pseudo-lettering.
12. **Negative Constraints:** Zero cartoon styling, zero 3D vector renders, zero surreal/floating elements, zero watermarks, and zero artificial plastic sheen.
13. **Target Resolution:** 4K master specifications (2880x2160, 3840x2160, 2048x1536) preserving optimal native clarity.
