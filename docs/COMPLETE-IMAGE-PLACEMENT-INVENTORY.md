## Executive Summary & Photography Art Direction
This inventory document establishes the complete, production-grade visual specification for every image placement across the BRNDNUR Advisory LLP website. Under the directive for **Ultra-Photorealistic Commercial Photography**, all generic graphics, 3D renders, and duplicated stock visuals are replaced with bespoke editorial and commercial photography briefs.

### Key Directives & Standards
1. **Zero Content Photo Reuse:** Every service card, hero section, and advisory feature receives an exclusive, purpose-crafted photograph. No single content photograph is shared across different routes.
2. **Commercial Photoshoot Realism:** Every prompt specifies professional camera bodies (Hasselblad H6D-100c, Leica SL2, Canon EOS R5, Sony A1, Nikon Z9, Fujifilm GFX 100), authentic focal lengths (35mm, 50mm, 85mm, 90mm macro), true physical lighting (natural morning window light, diffused key lighting), and tactile material textures (ivory cotton paper, cast bronze, dark navy linen, warm Kerala teakwood).
3. **Authentic Regional Context:** Portrayals of founders, advisors, and workshops authentically reflect the contemporary business landscape of Kerala and broader India—avoiding staged corporate clichés, synthetic skin smoothing, or artificial expressions.
4. **Exact Layout Alignment:** Aspect ratios (16:9 wide banners, 4:3 cards and heroes, 3:2 environmental portraits, 4:5 vertical splits) strictly reflect the real CSS container specifications, eliminating layout shift (CLS = 0.000).
