Frequent Questions

Questions Before You Get Started?

A few helpful answers before you decide on your next step.

Which service should I start with?

Tell us about your brand name and business type. We can help you understand which service or next step may be relevant to your situation.

What information should I prepare?

It is helpful to have your brand name, a short description of your goods or services, and basic applicant or business details. The exact requirements depend on the service.

How does a preliminary trademark search work?

A preliminary search checks for potentially relevant existing marks. It is an initial review, not a guarantee that an application will be accepted or registered.

How are service fees determined?

Fees depend on the service, applicant category, applicable government charges, and work involved. We will discuss the scope and relevant charges before proceeding.

Can BRNDNUR help with business registrations?

We provide guidance on GST, Udyam/MSME, FSSAI, and relevant business setup requirements, depending on your circumstances.
