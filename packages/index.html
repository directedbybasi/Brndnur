Frequently asked questions

Questions About Packages & Pricing?

A few helpful answers before you choose your next step.

Are government fees included in the package price?

Not necessarily. Ask for a quotation that clearly separates government charges from BRNDNUR's professional fees and any additional services.

Does trademark pricing depend on the number of classes?

Yes, the number of classes and your applicant category can affect the overall cost. Confirm the exact scope before proceeding.

What is included in the Food Brand Package?

The library describes trademark, Udyam/MSME and FSSAI-related support. Confirm current inclusions and which registrations apply to your business.

Can I get a package customised for my business?

Contact BRNDNUR to discuss your requirements and request a scope-specific quotation.

Are renewal and Brand Watch services included?

These may be separate services unless your written quotation states they are included.

How do I get a quotation before proceeding?

Share your brand name, business type and service needs over WhatsApp. Review the scope and charges before confirming.
