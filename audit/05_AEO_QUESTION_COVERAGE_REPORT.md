## 1. High-Priority Answer Blocks & Definitions

### Definition 1: Trademark vs. Company Name
> "Registering a company, LLP, or GST establishes your legal commercial entity and tax compliance, but does not grant proprietary trademark protection over your brand name. Trademark registration is a separate statutory intellectual property procedure under the Trade Marks Act 1999."

### Definition 2: Wordmark vs. Logo Registration
> "A wordmark protects the brand name itself in standard text, offering broad protection regardless of typography or styling. A logo (device mark) protects the distinctive visual symbol, stylized lettering, or emblem. Many businesses file both to secure comprehensive coverage."

### Definition 3: Government Fees Breakdown
> "Statutory official government fees are prescribed by the Trade Marks Registry (with a 50% concession for individuals, startups, and MSMEs: ₹4,500 per class vs. ₹9,000 for standard entities). BRNDNUR provides an itemised breakdown of official fees and advisory charges upfront."
