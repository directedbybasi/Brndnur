# Image Prompt Library — Page-Specific Concepts

Use these concepts to generate new original artwork when required. Prompts are briefs; tune aspect ratio/crop to the actual HTML container. Never reuse the same result across unrelated pages. No text/logos baked into images.

## Trademark / brand identity
- **Trademark registration hero:** cream portfolio with generic gold circled mark, navy law books, black-gold pen, marble desk, warm window shadows; horizontal composition with room for text if used in a blend.
- **Brand name search:** brass magnifying glass over blank cream document sheets, navy folder and pen; no web UI or legible text.
- **Wordmark/logo:** elegant typography proof sheets and neutral logo sketches, black-gold pen; avoid legible brand-like text.
- **Renewal:** organized neutral files, dated-calendar shape without readable date, navy leather book, gold pen; no claim of official certificate.
- **Monitoring/Brand Watch:** magnifying glass, layered folders and subtle paper tabs, navy/cream/gold palette; no fabricated notification text.
- **Trademark classes:** orderly category dividers and blank goods/services sheets, overhead editorial angle; no fake full class list.

## Business registration
- **GST:** neutral accountant desk, calculator and organized blank papers; do not depict fake GST certificate or official logo.
- **Udyam/MSME:** small business planning workspace with notebook, packaging sample, generic documents; no government badge.
- **FSSAI:** polished food-business production/packaging still life; no fake license/certificate, food-safe claims or official logo.
- **Business setup:** startup paperwork, notebook, architecture/interior workspace, desktop tools, warm light.

## Packages
- **Brand Starter Pack:** navy leather folio, gold emblem shape, brand notebook and pen; neutral and introductory.
- **Trademark Package:** ivory portfolio, trademark-style abstract seal, legal books; avoid readable invented document text.
- **Food Brand Package:** premium food product packaging, jars or bakery item, blank kraft/cream labels; no fake text on label.
- **Gulf Return Business Package:** premium international entrepreneur desk with skyline/travel cue only if tasteful; don't rely on flags or stereotyped imagery.
- **Pricing breakdown:** calculator, blank fee worksheet, pen, folio; values belong in HTML table.
- **Brand Watch/add-ons:** navy folio, magnifier and document folders.

## Main pages
- **About:** warm workspace with chair/desk and natural daylight; no fake staff portraits.
- **How It Works:** documents in sequence and a pen over a checklist, no readable fake checklist text.
- **FAQs:** navy/cream legal portfolio or books with restrained props.
- **Contact:** welcoming professional workspace with notebook and plant; no people or invented office sign.
- **Resources:** calm editorial desk with books and paper; use image only if it doesn't crowd the article.

## Prompt suffix (append to most briefs)
“Photorealistic high-end editorial photography; BRNDNUR’s cream, navy and muted gold palette; warm natural light; balanced composition with clear focal point; high detail; no people; no readable text; no actual logo; no watermark; no fake official certificate; no UI screenshot; suitable crop for [specified aspect ratio].”
