# Page Specs E — Resources and Educational Pages (Routes 23–27)

## General requirements for all resources
- Write for small-business owners using plain English and clear steps.
- Verify legal/trademark/tax facts with current official sources and link/cite them near the relevant claims.
- Show a publication/update date only when it is true and maintained. Do not invent authors, credentials, success examples, or article metadata.
- Include a brief “general information, not individual legal/tax advice” note when appropriate.
- Use one topic-specific hero visual at most; long educational pages should prioritize readable content, table of contents and references over decorative imagery.
- Include relevant internal links and one contextual CTA. No fake search/filter UI or empty article cards.

## 23. Resources / Blog `/resources/`
**Purpose:** find useful learning materials.
**Sections:** compact hero; category navigation (Trademark Basics, Applications, Brand Name Searches, Business Registration only if real content exists); featured guide(s); article list for the four planned guides; CTA to Services/Contact.
**Rules:** every card links to a real included article. Do not add fake blog titles, author names, publish dates, categories or search feature unless operational.

## 24. Trademark Registration Guide `/resources/trademark-registration-guide/`
**Article structure:** quick summary; what registration is; what to prepare; preliminary search and class choice; broad application process; what examination/objection/opposition can mean (verify); post-application monitoring and renewal; common mistakes; official source links; related services; disclaimer and CTA.
**Tone:** explain processes and uncertainty, not individual outcomes. No fixed approval timeline or guarantees.

## 25. Trademark Class Guide `/resources/trademark-class-guide/`
**Article structure:** what classes mean; goods vs services; why exact offerings matter; how to think about examples; class-selection checklist; common mistakes; link to `/trademark-classes/`; official classification source links; CTA.
**Rules:** examples must be verified before publication. Do not present an unverified exhaustive list or treat class examples as legal determination.

## 26. Brand Name Search Guide `/resources/brand-name-search-guide/`
**Article structure:** why search first; exact and similar names; spelling/sound/meaning/visual similarities where relevant; how goods/services/class affects interpretation; information to prepare; understanding findings; what a preliminary search cannot guarantee; next steps; official search source; CTA.
**Avoid:** “clear means guaranteed available” or claims that all identical/similar marks across all contexts have been excluded.

## 27. Trademark Application Process `/resources/trademark-application-process/`
**Article structure:** preparation; mark and applicant details; goods/services and class; application; examination; potential queries/objections/opposition or publication stages if applicable; monitoring status; registration/maintenance where relevant; official source links; related service links.
**Rules:** verify current procedure from official sources, explain that routes/status vary, avoid definitive processing timelines and guaranteed acceptance.
