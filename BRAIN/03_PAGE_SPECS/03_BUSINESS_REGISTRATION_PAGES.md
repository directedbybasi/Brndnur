# Page Specs C — Business Registration Pages (Routes 15–18)

These pages explain assistance, not official government authority. Each route must be verified against current official sources before publishing detailed requirements, thresholds, categories, deadlines or prices. Avoid implying that requirements are identical for every business.

## 15. GST Registration Assistance `/gst-registration/`
**Audience:** businesses exploring whether GST registration applies and how to navigate the process.
**Sections:**
1. Hero: “GST Registration Guidance for Your Business.”
2. When a business may need to consider GST registration — do not state a universal threshold without verified caveats.
3. Information typically needed: business constitution, PAN/contact details, address/proof and business activity as applicable; verify official checklist for current requirements.
4. Support process: initial review; identify applicable path; prepare information; follow official process; communicate status only when known.
5. Fee clarity: distinguish official government charge (if any) from BRNDNUR's professional charge; don't imply government fee is always zero or always payable.
6. What is included/excluded, FAQs, CTA.
**Visual:** business documents/accounting workspace, not tax-office seals or fake certificates.

## 16. Udyam / MSME Registration `/udyam-msme-registration/`
**Sections:** hero; purpose of Udyam; who may qualify based on current official definitions; information to prepare; overview of application/support; government registration fee vs professional assistance fee distinction; related potential uses without promise of any benefit; FAQs; CTA.
**Rules:** verify current Udyam portal instructions and eligibility. Do not promise every applicant qualifies for all schemes/loans or suggest a registration automatically guarantees benefits. Do not say BRNDNUR's fee is a government fee.

## 17. FSSAI Registration Assistance `/fssai-registration/`
**Audience:** food-related businesses seeking help understanding applicable registration/licensing routes.
**Sections:** hero; why requirements matter for food businesses; business/activity-specific categories; commonly requested information; process; fee and scope clarification; checklist by business stage if verified; FAQs; CTA.
**Rules:** verify current FSSAI/FoSCoS requirements and categories. Do not state all food businesses require the same license. Do not invent processing duration, government fee or guaranteed approval. Use food-specific imagery, not generic trademark books.

## 18. Business Setup Advisory `/business-setup/`
**Goal:** position BRNDNUR as a guide to understanding relevant registration/advisory steps, not a government office or all-inclusive legal/accounting firm unless documented.
**Sections:** hero; what “business setup support” means; discovery of business type/activity/locations; possible topics (GST, Udyam/MSME, FSSAI for applicable food businesses, trademark/brand protection); clarify that exact registrations depend on circumstances; work scope and quote; process; FAQs; CTA.
**CTA:** “Discuss Your Business Setup.” Link to Packages and relevant registration pages.
**Visual:** tidy startup/business documentation scene. Avoid fake company certificates or unsupported “complete compliance guaranteed” claims.
