# Page Specs B — Dedicated Trademark Pages (Routes 9–14)

## 9. Trademark Classes `/trademark-classes/`
**First action:** search the workspace and uploads for the earlier Trademark Classes HTML the owner mentioned. If found, inspect it and preserve valuable original content. If not found, note the result and build this spec. Never claim an earlier version was imported unless actually present.
**Goal:** help visitors understand class selection as part of an application, without pretending to make official decisions.
**Section flow:**
1. Hero: “Find the Right Trademark Class for Your Goods or Services” with CTA to ask for guidance.
2. Explain the purpose of classes in plain English.
3. Distinguish goods from services; verify examples and official classification terminology.
4. Examples by business type in a table/cards. Each example must be checked against official class information before publishing; explicitly label examples as indicative.
5. What affects class choice: actual goods/services, business scope and planned coverage; do not assume the company industry alone determines class.
6. How class selection fits into the application process.
7. Short checklist of information customers should prepare.
8. FAQs.
9. CTA to `/brand-name-search/` and `/trademark-registration/`.
**Images:** a class-specific desk/document photo only if it materially helps; otherwise use category icons and an examples table.

## 10. Brand Name Search `/brand-name-search/`
**Goal:** encourage a preliminary enquiry while setting clear expectations about what a search can and cannot establish.
**Section flow:**
1. Hero: “Check Your Brand Name Before You Move Forward.” CTA: request a preliminary search.
2. Why search is useful: find potentially relevant identical/similar marks and potential risk flags.
3. What information is needed: exact name/spelling, business/goods/services, intended market, applicant info only when required.
4. How it works: details received; search scope/classes clarified; potential results reviewed; options discussed.
5. Results explained: no obvious conflict found / potential similar marks / needs further review. Do not use “clear” as a legal guarantee.
6. Limitations: search scope matters; similarity and registrability involve judgment; official authority decides the application.
7. What happens next: link to trademark registration and classes.
8. FAQs and CTA.
**Visual:** new image around a magnifying glass/search paperwork or an understated search workspace; do not create a fake official portal screenshot.

## 11. Wordmark Registration `/wordmark-registration/`
**Goal:** explain a trademark application centered on a word/name element.
**Section flow:** hero; what a wordmark means; when to explore a wordmark; wordmark vs logo comparison; class/search considerations; information/document checklist; overview of application stages; pricing enquiry; FAQs; related links and CTA.
**Key distinction:** wordmark and logo applications are not interchangeable; the scope depends on the application, mark and applicable rules. Avoid promises about all stylizations or future uses being protected.
**CTA:** discuss a brand name or request a preliminary search.

## 12. Logo Trademark Registration `/logo-trademark-registration/`
**Goal:** explain support for an application involving a visual logo/mark.
**Section flow:** hero; what a logo mark is; prepare the correct logo artwork/version; applicant and goods/services details; application overview; wordmark-vs-logo comparison; separate applications/fees may be relevant depending on strategy; FAQs; quote CTA.
**Rules:** do not invent required pixel dimensions, file types or colour/mono requirements; verify official filing requirements. Do not claim a logo application automatically protects every variation, wordmark or future redesign.
**Visual:** a brand proof/document still life; never create a fake official certificate with legible false details.

## 13. Trademark Renewal `/trademark-renewal/`
**Goal:** explain renewal assistance for an existing registration.
**Section flow:** hero; why dates matter; details needed (mark, registration number/date and current status); process of record check, scope/fee review, preparation and filing support; explain government/professional fee separation; next-step checklist; FAQs; CTA.
**Rules:** dates, statutory periods, grace periods, forms, government charges and eligibility must be checked with current official sources. Do not calculate client-specific expiry from an assumed date. Do not guarantee acceptance or continuity.

## 14. Trademark Monitoring `/trademark-monitoring/`
**Goal:** describe the actual Brand Watch/monitoring offering accurately.
**Section flow:** hero; why monitoring may help; what is monitored and frequency (only what service actually does); what a report/alert includes (confirm with owner); what is excluded (filing oppositions, legal action, takedowns, enforcement unless confirmed); scope and annual quotation; FAQs; CTA.
**Pricing:** source template says Brand Watch ₹3,000–₹5,000 per year. Label as indicative only until owner confirms current pricing, coverage, frequency and deliverables.
**No claims:** continuous 24/7 monitoring, detection of every conflict, automatic enforcement/takedown or guaranteed prevention unless written service scope proves it.
