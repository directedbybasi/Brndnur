# Page Specs F — Legal and System Pages (Routes 28–32)

Legal/policy copy depends on actual business practices. These pages are **drafts requiring owner and, where appropriate, qualified legal review**. Don't invent terms to make the document look complete. Use clearly marked `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]` notes in working drafts, and remove them only after confirmation.

## 28. Privacy Policy `/privacy-policy/`
**Purpose:** disclose the actual data practices of this website/business.
**Sections:** policy title and effective/update date; data collected (only what forms/analytics/contact channels actually collect); how enquiries through WhatsApp/phone/email are handled; purposes; sharing/service providers; retention; security; cookies/analytics only if actually used; user choices/rights as applicable; children's data if relevant; contact for privacy queries.
**Before finalizing:** inspect the real site code for trackers/forms/cookies and ask the owner about storage, CRM, email services, retention and access. Do not state a tool is or isn't used without checking. Legal review recommended.

## 29. Terms & Conditions `/terms-and-conditions/`
**Sections:** scope of site information; allowed use; enquiry vs engagement; quotations and their validity only if defined by owner; payment and service commencement only as confirmed; customer responsibility for accurate details; third-party/government authority outcomes; IP/site content; limitations; changes; governing-law/venue only after confirmation; contacts.
**Rules:** don't invent refund exclusions, binding timelines, arbitration clauses or jurisdiction not approved by owner/legal reviewer.

## 30. Disclaimer `/disclaimer/`
**Sections:** general information purpose; the site is not an official government portal; preliminary searches are not guarantees; authority decisions control outcomes; service scope depends on the engagement; information can change; users should obtain situation-specific professional advice as needed; confirmed contact details.
**Do not say:** BRNDNUR is a law firm, licensed legal practice, government representative or guarantee provider unless this is independently confirmed and owner-approved. Describe advisory role accurately.

## 31. Refund & Cancellation Policy `/refund-cancellation-policy/`
**Must cover after owner confirmation:** enquiry vs accepted engagement; cancellation method; service work already performed; government fees/third-party costs; package-specific exceptions; refund calculation; method and processing period; contact route; effective/update date.
**Do not invent:** “no refund under any circumstances,” refund windows, fixed processing days, or automatic exclusions. Mark unresolved parts `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]` in drafts; legal review required before publication.

## 32. Custom 404 `/404.html`
**Purpose:** recover gracefully when URL is missing.
**Layout:** same header/footer; compact friendly headline (“We couldn’t find that page”); one-sentence explanation; buttons for Home, Services, Packages and Contact; optional service links. Avoid fake search box unless it searches real content.
**Technical:** verify host supports a custom 404 and responds with HTTP 404, not 200, when deployed. On local file preview it may not behave identically.
