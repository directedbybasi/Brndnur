## Requirements that apply to every page
- One purpose, one unique H1, a descriptive title and meta description, a primary CTA, relevant internal links, shared header/footer, and responsive layout.
- Copy must be concise, plain professional English. No text walls. Use useful headings, bullets, comparison tables or step lists when they make information easier to scan.
- Use specific content sections and image concepts for this route. Do not simply clone another page and change its H1.
- Every visible CTA must go somewhere useful. Package/service CTA messages should reflect the route.
- Read `02_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `04_CONTENT_PRICING_AND_TRUST.md`, `05_COMPONENTS_AND_INTERACTIONS.md`, and `09_NAVIGATION_CONTACTS_AND_LINKS.md` as relevant.
